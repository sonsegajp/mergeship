# Unified Mergeship randomizer: design (2026-09-23)

One seed over Hyrule and Termina, generated by SoH's generator. MM's world graph is translated into SoH's logic
framework, with MM's three-day time model embedded as a time solver. The seed file has one section per engine, each
in the format that engine already reads. Found items go to a shared inventory owned by the host.

## Why SoH's generator
SoH's generator already has assumed fill, a sphere playthrough, WotH/barren, hint areas, entrance shuffle,
plandomizer, a settings UI, a tracker and Anchor.
- 2Ship's generator does forward fill with junk replacement, and its solver state is MM's own `gSaveContext`
  (`Logic/GlitchlessLogic.cpp`).
- Two generators plus a coordinator can't prove beatability when items cross between the worlds.

## Current structure
**SoH**
- Pipeline: `3drando/menu.cpp:14` GenerateRandomizer → `playthrough.cpp:17` → `fill.cpp:1274` Fill.
- Reachability: `ProcessRegion` at `fill.cpp:460`.
- Logic: regions are lambdas in `location_access/*`, the graph is assembled in `RegionTable_Init`
  (`location_access.cpp:871`), and item logic lives in `logic.cpp` (`HasItem`/`CanUse`/`ApplyItemEffect`).
- Spoiler: `3drando/spoiler_log.cpp`, parsed back by `Context::ParseSpoiler` (`SeedContext.cpp:405`).
- Runtime: `GetFinalGIEntry` (`SeedContext.cpp:376`), `Randomizer_Item_Give` (`randomizer.cpp:1149`),
  `RandomizerOnItemReceiveHandler` (`hook_handlers.cpp:287`).
- Save: the `"randomizer"` section.

**2Ship**
- Generation: at file creation, in `MiscBehavior/OnFileCreate.cpp`.
- Logic: `Logic/Logic.h` (RandoRegion; macros `HAS_ITEM`, `CAN_BE_DEKU`, `CAN_PLAY_SONG`, ... at lines 178-243;
  `CanKillEnemy` at 617-780) and `Logic/Regions/*.cpp` (16 files, about 6.5k lines).
- Time model: 45 slices, in `TimeLogic.cpp`.
- Tables: `Types.h` (RandoCheckId about 2643, RandoItemId about 240), `StaticData/Checks.cpp`, `Items`, `Options`.
- Runtime: `CheckQueue.cpp:29-99` → `GiveItem` / `ConvertItem`.
- Spoiler: type `2S2H_RANDO_SPOILER`, in `Spoiler/*`.
- Save: `RandoSaveInfo`, in `include/z64save.h:372-393`.

## Porting MM's logic into SoH
- **Translation script:** `tools/port_mm_rando.py` (re-runnable, because 2Ship upstream moves). It translates
  2Ship's regions into `location_access/termina/*.cpp`. Enum names collide between the two games, as do item
  values (ITEM_HOOKSHOT is 0x0A in OoT but 0x0F in MM), so the script rewrites them to prefixed forms
  (`RC_MM_*`, `RR_MM_*`, `LOGIC_MM_*`, `mm->Has(MMI_*)`).
- **World tag:** Location, Region and ItemLocation get a world field.
- **Time solver:** MM regions carry a 45-bit time mask. `ProcessRegion` runs the ported `ExpandTimeForward` and
  `SetCurrentRegionTime` before it evaluates conditions.
- **Age:** MM regions are age-neutral.
- **Door:** a region in `lost_woods.cpp` exits to `RR_MM_CLOCK_TOWER_INTERIOR`, and the return exit comes back.
  2Ship's pseudo-root `RR_MAX` becomes `RR_MM_ROOT` (the save warp and owl soaring).
- **Item effects:** `TerminaLogic::ApplyItem` reimplements the logic-relevant side effects of 2Ship's
  `GiveItem.cpp` and `ConvertItem.cpp`, such as week-event bits and RandoInf flags.

## Shared item model
- **Canonical id:** `include/mergeship/MsItems.def` (an X-macro) lists, for each item: key, RandomizerGet,
  RandoItemId, kind, persistence (permanent or MM-cycle), usable in OoT / usable in MM, and max level.
- **Ownership vs use:** ownership is shared. Usability is per engine, and logic counts an item in a world only if
  that engine supports it.
- **Swords:** the progressive chain is Kokiri → Razor → Gilded. Master, Biggoron and Great Fairy's stay separate.
- **Shields:** MM's Hero's Shield maps to the Hylian Shield, and the Mirror Shield is one shared item.
- **Hearts:** one budget, capped at 20.
- **Bottles:** a shared count capped at 6; OoT shows 4.
- **Tokens and fairies:** OoT tokens, MM swamp tokens, MM ocean tokens and stray fairies stay separate counters.
- **Win condition:** Ganon and/or Majora, or a Triforce hunt across both worlds.

## Delivery and reporting
- **Seed:** one `MERGESHIP_SEED` JSON: `{shared, oot: <SoH spoiler>, mm: <2S2H spoiler body>, hints, playthrough}`.
- **Generation:** runs in SoH's DLL through `Mergeship_GenerateSeed()`. The host stores the result in the shared
  save's `mergeship.rando` section.
- **Host API:** `include/mergeship/MsHostApi.h` with `OnItemObtained`, `ReportCheck`, `GetLevel` and
  `Subscribe(projector)`.
- **Reporting points:** SoH `hook_handlers.cpp:491`, `randomizer.cpp:1149` and `SeedContext.cpp:376`; 2Ship
  `CheckQueue.cpp:94` and `GiveItem.cpp`.
- **Projection:** each engine projects the shared inventory into its native save, idempotently, on every change
  and on every door switch.
- **Anchor:** packet `{msItemId, world, checkKey}` in the shared layer.

## Settled decisions (M0, 2026-09-23)
The table is `include/mergeship/MsItems.def` in the MergeShip2 repository (with `MsItems.h`). Every SoH
RandomizerGet and every 2Ship RandoItemId is in it exactly once (static_asserts in `soh/MergeShip/MergeShipMsItems.cpp`
and `2s2h/Rando/MergeShip/UnifiedSeed.cpp`; `tools/port_mm_rando.py --check`).
- **Identity:** an item's key is its RandomizerGet without `RG_`. Shop "Buy" variants and MergeShip-mode MM gear are
  aliases (`MS_ALIAS_RG`) of the item they give: `RG_MM_HEROS_SHIELD` = Hylian Shield, `RG_MM_MIRROR_SHIELD` = Mirror
  Shield, `RG_MM_HEROS_BOW` = Fairy Bow.
- **Proxies:** the 240 items 2Ship has no RandoItemId for (Ocarina of Time's, and the MM bottles SoH added) get
  `RI_OOT_<key>`, appended after `RI_MAX_TRAP` so no saved id moves. 2Ship gives a found proxy natively where it has
  the item and writes it to the shared ledger for Hyrule ("Delivery (M3)" below).
- **Swords:** Kokiri (`RG_KOKIRI_SWORD` = `RI_SWORD_KOKIRI`), then `RG_PROGRESSIVE_MM_SWORD` = `RI_PROGRESSIVE_SWORD`
  twice (Razor, Gilded): the chain's level is Kokiri owned + progressive copies. Master, Biggoron's (and Giant's Knife)
  and the Great Fairy's Sword are separate items. The pool's third progressive copy (2Ship adds one with no location)
  becomes junk.
- **Shared progressives:** bow and quivers, bomb bags, wallets (2Ship's Tycoon = SoH's), magic, ocarina (MM's is
  level 1 of `RG_PROGRESSIVE_OCARINA`), ocarina buttons, swim (`RI_ABILITY_SWIM` = `RG_BRONZE_SCALE`), lens, the
  three magic arrows, Double Defense, Skeleton Key, and the songs both games have (Time, Epona, Storms, Sun, Saria).
  MM's Hookshot stays its own item (it has Longshot reach); Hyrule's hookshots are usable in MM through it.
- **Masks:** Ocarina of Time's Keaton, Bunny, Truth (and Goron, Zora) are separate from MM's; MM's 24 masks are
  `RG_MM_*`.
- **Hearts:** one budget of 20, Hyrule's. Termina's heart pieces and containers enter the unified pool as junk.
- **Bottles:** at most 6 bottle items in the unified pool (Hyrule's first); Termina's beyond that become junk. OoT
  shows bottles 1-4, MM 1-6. Contents are consumables.
- **Consumables on MM's cycle reset:** `MS_CYCLE` items (ammo, rupees, refills, bottle contents, magic beans) are not
  shared. They go to the engine whose check gave them, and Song of Time resets MM's counts by MM's rules; Hyrule's are
  untouched. Everything `MS_PERM` is shared and survives the reset; trade items and dungeon keys persist as 2Ship's
  randomizer already keeps them.
- **Redundant copies:** any `MS_PERM` item with a `maxLevel` that the pool already holds that many of becomes junk
  (e.g. MM's Lens when Hyrule's pool has one).
- **Usability** (`oot` / `mm` columns) is intent for logic (M4): 1 where the engine can use the item natively or
  through the native item its projector gives. It follows the engines as they gain items (the OoT items 2Ship's
  `MergeShipOoTItems.h` adds are already 1).

## Implementation (M0-M2, 2026-09-23)
- **Setting:** `RSK_MERGESHIP_TERMINA` (CVar `gRandoSettings.MergeShipTermina`, off). Off, a seed is exactly what it
  was: no Termina location is in the pool, no region or exit is added, the Termina settings are neither hashed
  (`MergeShip_Termina_SettingCounts`, playthrough.cpp) nor written to the spoiler, and nothing consumes RNG.
- **Port:** `tools/port_mm_rando.py` (MergeShip2 repo, re-runnable, `--check` verifies) generates `RC_MM_*` (2641
  locations, `Location::Termina`, world `RW_TERMINA`, spoiler names "MM ..."), the 115 `RG_MM_*` items SoH lacked,
  `RR_MM_*` (318 regions and `RR_MM_ROOT`), `RSK_MM_*` (2Ship's options; three that do not fit an 8-bit option pass
  through with 2Ship's default), `location_list_termina.cpp`, `item_list_termina.cpp`, `settings_termina.inc`,
  `location_access/termina/root.cpp` and `soh/MergeShip/MergeShipTerminaData.cpp` (per check: 2Ship name, type,
  scene, vanilla item, home region).
- **World tag:** `Location::GetWorld`, `Region::world`, `ItemLocation::GetWorld`. Termina locations are skipped by the
  check tracker, the menus' location lists and exclusions, and are not hintable yet.
- **Door:** `RR_LW_TERMINA_DOOR` (lost_woods.cpp), reached from `RR_THE_LOST_WOODS` by the exit that loops back to
  Kokiri Forest (the host's M3 handoff), exits to `RR_MM_CLOCK_TOWER_INTERIOR`, which leads to `RR_MM_ROOT` holding
  every Termina location with no condition (M1, no logic); both ways, both ages. Termina's regions have area `RA_NONE`.
- **Pools:** `MergeShip_Termina_PrepareLocationPool` follows 2Ship's GeneratePools (type options, songs, remains,
  shops and their prices, partial skulltulas, vanilla dungeon items, Majora's pots); `MergeShip_Termina_AddItemsToPool`
  adds the vanilla items and 2Ship's extras (souls, clocks, swim, buttons, songs, Tycoon, Skeleton Key), removes
  2Ship's computed starting items and extra stray fairies, applies the caps above and trims to the location count.
- **Seed file:** with the setting on, SpoilerLog_Write writes one `MERGESHIP_SEED`: `oot` is the SoH spoiler (SoH's
  ParseSpoiler unwraps it), `mm` a 2S2H spoiler body (options by RandoOptionId name, checks by RandoCheckId name,
  items by RandoItemId name, prices for shops), `shared` has the table version and count, the door and every
  placement (`world`, 2Ship/SoH check, item key), `hints` and `playthrough` are SoH's (the fill is one fill).
- **2Ship (M2):** `Spoiler/File.cpp` and `Apply.cpp` take a `MERGESHIP_SEED` and use its `mm` section (checking
  version and table), `HandleFileDropped.cpp` accepts one, and `MiscBehavior/OnFileCreate.cpp` applies the seed named
  by CVar `gMergeShip.Rando.SeedFile` (set by the host) instead of generating.
- **Evidence:** `build/tests/rando_unified_m1` (harness `randogen` + `randoterm`, and `check.py`), and
  `build/tests/rando_unified_baseline` to show an "off" seed equals the pre-change build's.

### Goals (2026-09-23)
`soh/MergeShip/MergeShipGoal.h` (SoH) and `2s2h/Rando/MergeShip/UnifiedGoal.h` (2Ship); settings in the randomizer
menu's "Mergeship" sidebar (`settings_mergeship_goal.inc`), all Termina settings (after `RSK_MERGESHIP_TERMINA`).
- **Settings:** `RSK_MS_GOAL` Ganon / Majora / Ganon and Majora (default) / Triforce Hunt; `RSK_MS_TRIFORCE_PIECES_TOTAL`
  and `_REQUIRED` (1-100, pieces anywhere in either world); `RSK_MS_REMAINS_AS_MEDALLIONS` (MM's remains count as
  medallions for the bridge, Ganon's Boss Key, Ganon's Soul and SoH's win-condition triggers); `RSK_MS_MAJORA_FORM_MASKS`
  (transformation masks on top of 2Ship's 20-mask count). 2Ship's own `RSK_MM_ACCESS_*` moon and Majora options are in
  the same sidebar. "Ganon" is SoH's own win condition (Ganon, or one of SoH's triggers; "Anywhere" counts as Ganon).
- **Generation:** unless the goal is "Ganon", the Triforce is at `RC_WINCON` and Ganon gives junk; `CanTriggerWincon`
  is `MergeShip_Goal_LogicMet`: `LOGIC_MS_GANON_DEFEATED` (Ganon's arena, unified seeds only) or SoH's trigger, and
  `LOGIC_MM_MAJORA_DEFEATED` (set by the Termina port in `RR_MM_MOON_MAJORAS_LAIR`; while that region is empty, M1,
  Majora counts once Termina is reached with the remains and masks 2Ship asks for), or the pieces. The remains and masks
  a goal needs are advancement.
- **Seed:** `shared.winCondition` and `shared.goal`; the `mm` section sets `RO_TRIFORCE_PIECES_REQUIRED` out of reach
  (2Ship's own hunt never ends the game) and `RO_SHUFFLE_TRIFORCE_PIECES` 0.
- **In game:** the shared save's goal block (`goalType`, `goalFlags`, `majoraFormMasks`, `triforceRequired`,
  `goalSeed`): SoH writes the configuration and Hyrule's part, 2Ship Majora's; Triforce pieces count from the ledger.
  Ganon's defeat when it is not the whole goal leaves the castle with a message; Majora's defeat when it is not the whole
  goal takes Link back to the dawn of the first day (the Song of Time) with a message. The ending plays in the world
  where the goal completes. The adult altar and the Clock Tower rooftop say what the goal takes.
- **Harness scripts (not run yet):** `build/tests/rando_goals_gen` (check.py), `rando_goals_soh`, `rando_goals_2ship_majora`,
  `rando_goals_2ship_triforce`, `rando_goals_host`.

### Termina logic and pools (M4, 2026-09-23)
- **World graph:** `tools/port_mm_rando.py` parses 2Ship's `Logic/Regions/*.cpp` and `Logic.cpp` (each region's checks,
  exits, connections, events, one-way entrances, STAY restrictions, the files' local helpers) and writes one
  `location_access/termina/<file>.cpp` per 2Ship file plus `root.cpp` (`RR_MAX` as `RR_MM_ROOT`; the door lands in
  `RR_MM_CLOCK_TOWER_INTERIOR`, which also leads to the root). Exits go to entrance ids, resolved as 2Ship's
  `GetRegionIdFromEntrance` (an entrance no region returns through is the root, as in 2Ship); exits and connections
  to one region become one SoH exit. Conditions are rewritten token by token (`HAS_ITEM(ITEM_X)` ->
  `mm->Has(MMI_X)`, `RANDO_EVENTS[RE_X]` -> `logic->Get(LOGIC_MM_X)`, `BETWEEN(TIME_A, TIME_B)` ->
  `mm->Between(MMT_A, MMT_B)`, ...); an identifier the tool does not know fails `--check`. 2Ship's events are
  `LOGIC_MM_*` (`LogicValTermina.inc`); an event 2Ship counts (the Zora eggs, `>= 7`) gets one LogicVal per setter.
  Goals: `LOGIC_MM_ODOLWA/GOHT/GYORG/TWINMOLD_DEFEATED` (on the boss warp's condition) and `LOGIC_MM_MAJORA_DEFEATED`
  in Majora's lair (`CanDefeatMajora`).
- **Item state:** `soh/MergeShip/MergeShipTerminaLogic.h` (hand-written) is 2Ship's `Logic.h` over the unified model:
  counts per `MsItemKey`, reset with SoH's `Logic::Reset`, fed by `Logic::ApplyItemEffect`. Termina counts an item only
  if `mm` is 1, and a consumable (`MS_CYCLE`) only when a Termina location gave it (`ItemLocation::ApplyPlacedItemEffect`
  tells the source world); Hyrule's logic likewise skips items with `oot` 0 and Termina's consumables. 2Ship's
  GiveItem side effects are derived: week-event 08_80 = the Clock Town fairy, RandoInf deeds/letters/souls, owls, keys
  (Skeleton Key = all), progressive levels (sword chain Kokiri + progressive copies), frogs, clocks. Swim and ocarina
  buttons are innate unless 2Ship's own shuffle of them is on (then the shared Bronze Scale / button items).
  Every item it reads is advancement in a unified seed (`Item::IsAdvancement`).
- **Time solver:** `Region::mmTime` (45 bits, reset with the region). `ProcessRegion` runs the ported
  `ExpandTimeForward` (with the region's STAYs, clock shuffle's owned half-days) and makes the region's time current
  for its events, exits and locations; `ProcessExits` carries a Termina region's time through an exit whose condition
  holds at it (2Ship's `FindReachableRegions`); entering from Hyrule is Day 1 06:00 (the owned time with clock
  shuffle); the root always holds that and never waits. Hyrule's regions never touch it.
- **Pools:** in the unified mode every RSK_MM_* check shuffle defaults on (`UNIFIED_DEFAULTS` in the tool), boss remains
  "Anywhere": all 2639 checks 2Ship can shuffle (all but Majora's two pots) are in the pool. A check 2Ship keeps vanilla
  whose vanilla item is advancement (songs, remains, keys, owls, tokens...) is in SoH's pool holding that item, so the
  logic finds it; 2Ship leaves it vanilla (not in "mm" unless 2Ship lists it). 2Ship's own-dungeon placements
  (keys, boss keys, stray fairies, remains) and songs-on-song-checks (spare song checks get junk, Song of Healing's
  first) run in `Fill` after Hyrule's restricted pools (`MergeShip_Termina_PlaceRestrictedItems`). Clock shuffle's
  starting half-day is chosen by the seed and written to the "mm" section's `startingItems`.
- **Evidence:** `build/tests/rando_m4_logic` (script.txt, check.py; `randoterm` prints `M4POOL`, `M4TYPES`, `M4WORLD`,
  `M4PLAYTHROUGH`, `M4REACH`).

### Delivery (M3, 2026-09-23)
`include/mergeship/MsDelivery.h` (shared rules), SoH `soh/MergeShip/MergeShipUnified.h`, 2Ship
`2s2h/Rando/MergeShip/UnifiedDelivery.h`.
- **Ledger:** `SharedSave.h` version 2 appends `ledgerSeed`, `ledgerCount` and `ledger[8192]` (`MsLedgerEntry`: item
  key, check in its world's numbering, world, flags). The host reads a version 1 file as version 2 with an empty ledger.
  Every check found in either world while a unified seed plays is appended once (deduplicated by world + check) and
  committed at once: SoH on `OnRandoSetCheckStatus(RCSHOW_COLLECTED)` (and, on loading the file, every found check the
  ledger lacks), 2Ship in `CheckQueue`'s give and `EnGirlA`'s purchase. Trackers read `world` + `check` (M7). Loading a
  unified file claims its slot's ledger for its seed (`ledgerSeed` = `ctx->GetSeed()`, the `mm` section's `finalSeed`);
  another seed's ledger is cleared.
- **Routes (never both):** the finding engine gives the item natively when it can. The other engine gets it either
  from the M4 state sync (`MsDelivery_StateCarried`: masks, songs, capacities, magic, the sword chain after Kokiri,
  the Mirror Shield, bottles, Termina's items, trade slots, remains, stray fairies, the progressive ocarina) or from the
  ledger (`MS_LEDGER_FOR_HYRULE` / `_FOR_TERMINA`: everything else permanent, and anything the finder could not hold).
  Consumables (`MS_CYCLE`) stay with the finder; Triforce pieces are counted from the ledger by the goal and never
  given across (`MsDelivery_LedgerOnly`).
- **Projection:** each engine gives the other world's entries for it natively, in order, from a cursor saved in its
  own file (SoH section `mergeShipUnified.ledgerApplied`, 2Ship `ShipSaveInfo::mergeShipLedgerApplied`), when the
  player is free (not receiving an item, no textbox; 2Ship after MM's prologue), with a "From Hyrule: / From Termina:"
  notification. A native save that is not saved rolls its cursor back with its gives: projecting is idempotent.
- **2Ship proxies:** `Rando::GiveItem` gives an `RI_OOT_*` natively where 2Ship has it (MergeShipOoTItems: C-button
  items, OoT masks and trade items, Ruto's Letter; MergeShipOoTEquip: swords, shields, tunics, boots, strength,
  scales, bullet bags; MergeShipHyrule: songs, medallions, Spiritual Stones, Stone of Agony, Gerudo Card; new bottles,
  stick/nut capacities, the ocarina, OoT consumables). The get-item draws Ocarina of Time's model (or Majora's Mask's
  closest: keys, maps, compasses, tokens), the textbox uses OoT's icon, and an item Termina cannot use reads
  "You found X for Hyrule!". MM's one ocarina is level 1 of the progressive ocarina: a Termina copy found with it in
  hand becomes `RI_OOT_OCARINA_OF_TIME` for Hyrule (`Rando::ConvertItem`), and 2Ship captures its ocarina as
  `MS_ITEM_OCARINA_FAIRY` in a unified seed (any Hyrule ocarina lets Termina play).
- **SoH:** a generated Termina-only `RG_MM_*` gives nothing in `Randomizer_Item_Give` (no assert; Termina's consumables
  give their Ocarina of Time equivalent) and reads "You found X for Termina!". The shared state's Razor/Gilded Sword and
  Goron Lullaby set SoH's progressive found-flags so the next copy is the right one.
- **M4 projector fixes:** 2Ship's shared-save projector now carries the Sun's Song and Saria's Song and Ruto's Letter
  in a bottle.
- **Evidence (not run yet):** `build/tests/rando_m3_cross` (script_soh.txt, script_2ship.txt, check.py; harness commands
  `unified`, `ledger`, `ledgeradd`, `hyrulefind` / `terminafind`, `project`, `unifiedstate`).

## Entrances (M8, 2026-09-23)
User requirement: dungeon entrances, boss chambers and grottos shuffled between both games; grottos either per game or
across both. Three settings, each Off / Per Game / Cross-Game, only in a unified seed (Termina settings: hashed and
written only with `RSK_MERGESHIP_TERMINA`): `RSK_MERGESHIP_ER_DUNGEONS` (CVar `gRandoSettings.MergeShipERDungeons`),
`RSK_MERGESHIP_ER_BOSSES` (`...MergeShipERBosses`), `RSK_MERGESHIP_ER_GROTTOS` (`...MergeShipERGrottos`). No menu yet
(settings UI, M5).
- **Which:** dungeons -- the four temples, Inverted Stone Tower Temple, Beneath the Well, the Ancient Castle of Ikana;
  boss chambers -- Odolwa, Goht, Gyorg, Twinmold; grottos -- all 26 of 2Ship's (with the bean grotto and Lone Peak
  Shrine). `tools/gen_mm_entrances.py` (re-runnable, `--check`) derives everything else from 2Ship's regions and
  writes `soh/MergeShip/MergeShipTerminaEntranceData.inc`, `MergeShipTerminaTrackerData.inc` (tracker and spoiler
  names) and 2Ship's `2s2h/MergeShip/MergeShipEntranceData.inc`.
- **Keys** (`include/mergeship/MsEntrances.h`): Hyrule keeps its entrance indices; Termina's are `0x4000 | (MM
  entrance >> 4)`, `0x5000 | n` (leave MM grotto n), `0x5100 | boss` (the way back out of an MM lair, which MM does not
  have: a Hyrule boss room behind an MM boss door returns to that temple's entrance).
- **Generator:** `MergeShipEntrances.cpp` registers the pairs in `SetAllEntrancesData` (Per Game: types
  `TerminaDungeon/Boss/Grotto`, pools of their own; Cross-Game: Hyrule's types, so they share Hyrule's pools and
  SoH's own mixed-pool and decoupled options apply; Cross-Game switches SoH's matching option on). MM blue warps join
  SoH's blue warp rule (a warp leads where the dungeon standing at its dungeon's door has its own warp).
  `ENTRANCE_OVERRIDES_MAX_COUNT` is 400. A region the logic port has not built is stood in for (joined to
  `RR_MM_ROOT`), so the shuffle works before M4; with M4's regions nothing is stood in for.
- **Seed:** `MERGESHIP_SEED["entrances"] = {version, termina: [[key, override]...], hyrule: [...]}`; SoH also hands
  the loaded file's Termina overrides to 2Ship with every trip (`MsTransfer.entranceTable`,
  `MS_TRANSFER_FLAG_ENTRANCE_TABLE`); a trip through a shuffled entrance carries the key
  (`MS_TRANSFER_FLAG_RANDO_ENTRANCE`).
- **SoH runtime:** every override ends in `Grotto_OverrideSpecialEntrance`; a Termina key there becomes
  `ENTR_MERGESHIP_TERMINA` (the door's held transition) with the key kept; savewarps, game overs and blue warps that
  land on one load that Lost Woods entrance and leave after its fade-in. Arrivals from Termina are Ocarina of Time
  indices resolved as any override is (grotto returns get their respawn data).
- **2Ship runtime:** at the commit (`MergeShipHost_HoldTransition` -> `MergeShipEntrances_OnCommit`) the exit being
  taken is keyed (an exit of the table from its scene, a grotto hole via Door_Ana's respawn data, a grotto's way out,
  a lair's blue warp incl. the Giants' Chamber skip's bookkeeping); its override stays in Termina (replaced entrance)

## Hints and trackers (M6, M7, 2026-09-23)
- **Hyrule's hints over Termina** (`soh/MergeShip/MergeShipUnifiedHints.h`, setting `RSK_MS_TERMINA_HINTS` "Mergeship:
  Hints Cover Termina", CVar `gRandoSettings.MergeShip.TerminaHints`, default on; a Termina setting, hashed only in a
  unified seed): Termina gets 26 hint areas (`RA_MM_*`: Clock Town ... the Moon, the four temples, Beneath the Well,
  the Ancient Castle of Ikana as dungeons), assigned per 2Ship region by its scene (grottos and fairy fountains by
  region name) in `RegionTable_Init`. With areas, Termina's locations are hintable and fill.cpp's Way of the Hero and
  foolish weigh both worlds alike. An item hint about a Termina check reads "They say that #<check># in Termina holds
  #<item>#."; the generated `RG_MM_*` items are named by their item name (" (MM)"). Off: Termina keeps `RA_NONE`.
- **Mask of Truth** (`MergeShipMaskMessages.cpp`, CVar `gEnhancements.MergeShip.TruthTerminaHint`, default on): half of
  the stones' extra hints name one of Termina's major items and its area.
- **The Hyrule catalog** (`include/mergeship/MsWorldCatalog.h`, `MsTransfer.worldCatalog`): SoH sends every Hyrule
  check that holds an item of the seed (name, tracker area, MsItemKey, `MS_CATALOG_TRACKED` for what its check tracker
  shows) with every trip; 2Ship copies it on arrival (`2s2h/Rando/MergeShip/UnifiedHyrule.h`).
- **2Ship's hints over Hyrule** (CVar `gEnhancements.MergeShip.HintHyrule`, default on): the item hints (Oath, Soaring,
  Hookshot, remains, transformation masks, moon stones, gold dust) name the Hyrule check when Hyrule holds the item
  ("in Hyrule's Kokiri Forest"); Gossip Stones and purchasable hints draw Hyrule's checks too, weighted as 2Ship's.
- **Trackers** read the ledger live: SoH's check tracker lists Termina's tracked checks by Termina area (CVar
  `gEnhancements.MergeShip.TrackTermina`), its item tracker gets a "Termina Items Tracker" window (CVar
  `...ItemTrackerTermina`); 2Ship's check tracker lists Hyrule's checks by SoH's areas (CVar `...TrackHyrule`).

  `hintitem`, `truthhint`, `truthcandidates`, `trackterm`, `trackitems`, `catalog`; 2Ship `hyrulecatalog`,
